Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate HookForge. When you use the hook generator, we temporarily process your topic input to generate results — this data is not stored. If you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed password. Payment information is handled entirely by our third-party processor (Ko-fi) and never touches our servers.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to understand aggregate usage patterns — page views, session duration, and feature usage. This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally. You can opt out via your browser's Do Not Track setting or by using an ad blocker.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication (if you sign in) and optional analytics cookies. No third-party tracking cookies are used. Your data is not sold, shared, or monetized.

4. Data Retention

Hook generation data is ephemeral — it exists only in your browser session and is never stored on our servers. Account data (email + hashed password) is retained until you delete your account.

5. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing shubhumjosh@gmail.com. We will respond within 7 days.

6. Third-Party Services

HookForge uses OpenAI's API to generate hooks and scripts. The content you submit is processed by OpenAI solely for the purpose of generating results and is not used to train their models. See OpenAI's privacy policy for details.
